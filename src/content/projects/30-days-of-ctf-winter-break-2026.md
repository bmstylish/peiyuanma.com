## Project goal

This winter break, I am dedicating 30 days to polishing the core skills used in Capture the Flag competitions. The focus is not just collecting flags. I want to improve how I enumerate problems, test assumptions, use unfamiliar tools, recover when an approach fails, and explain a solution clearly afterward.

Each session is designed to take **60–90 minutes**:

- 10–15 minutes learning
- 45–60 minutes solving
- 10–15 minutes documenting the session
- Extra picoCTF challenges from the day's category if I finish early

### End target

- 60+ completed levels, labs, or challenges
- 10–15 written solutions
- One complete beginner machine
- One simulated or live CTF
- One specialty selected for deeper study next month
